Email Hasher

Hash email addresses locally with SHA-256

Step-by-step instructions

  1. Prepare your CSV — First row must be exactly email. One email per line below.
  2. Upload — Click the box or drag & drop your .csv file.
  3. Process — Click the button. Large files may take a minute.
  4. Download — Save the hashed CSV. Each row becomes a 64-character hex hash.
Example format (first 3 lines): 
email
user@example.com
other@domain.org

UTF-8 encoding. Emails are trimmed and lowercased before hashing. Output is Meta-compatible SHA-256.
